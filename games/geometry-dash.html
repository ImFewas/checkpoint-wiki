Controls basics

Jump

Tap, click, or press space/up. Holding it auto-jumps repeatedly in Cube mode — useful, but risky near tight spike clusters.

Ship / UFO / Wave

Same single input, different physics. Ship flies while held, UFO does discrete hops, Wave moves in a diagonal line while held.

Gravity portals

Yellow/pink orbs and portals flip gravity or change your mode mid-level. Learn the portal layout before you learn the jumps.

Practice mode use it

Practice mode places checkpoints as you play, so a death sends you back seconds instead of to the start. It's the single biggest reason people clear hard levels.

  • Place a checkpoint right before a section you keep failing, not right after.
  • Clear checkpoints and restart clean once you can consistently pass a section — muscle memory built with checkpoints can make the timing feel different in a normal run.
  • Practice runs don't count for stars or leaderboard completion — you still need a normal-mode clear.

Tips that actually helped beginner → intermediate

  • Listen, don't just look. Most levels are built so jump timing lines up with the beat. Once you feel the rhythm, blind spike clusters get much easier.
  • Learn transitions, not whole levels. Break a level into the 5–10 mode-change points and drill those individually.
  • Lower your in-game FPS assumptions. If your frame rate is unstable, timing feels inconsistent — a capped, stable frame rate is more reliable than an uncapped, jittery one.
  • Watch a clean run once, not repeatedly. One reference run for route knowledge is useful; watching it on loop tends to just build frustration.
When you're stuck for a while: step away rather than mashing the same attempt. Timing games punish tilt — a fresh five minutes later usually beats fifty frustrated attempts in a row.

Difficulty tiers reference

Official levels are rated by difficulty face and star count; user levels get rated after enough plays and a moderator review.

Auto Easy Normal Hard Harder Insane Demon (Easy → Extreme)

Demon-tier levels are a separate scale from the rest — an Easy Demon can still be much harder than a regular Insane level. Star rewards scale with difficulty and are the main progression metric outside of raw completion count.
